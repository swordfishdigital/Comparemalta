Lahmacun: what it is, where it comes from and three places to eat it in Malta

A paper-thin flatbread with spiced minced meat, rolled up with parsley and lemon. Its story runs from medieval Aleppo to Gaziantep, and these are three places that serve it in Malta.

By CompareMalta.ai

Photos: Rainer Zenz, CC BY-SA 3.0, Wikimedia Commons

Lahmacun is a very thin, round flatbread spread with minced meat (usually beef or lamb), onion, tomato, peppers and herbs, then baked fast in a very hot oven. It is often called "Turkish pizza", but there is no cheese and no sauce, and the base is much thinner. The classic way to eat it: squeeze over lemon, add parsley and onion, roll it up and eat it with your hands, often with a glass of ayran.

The name comes from the Arabic laḥm bi-ʿajīn, "meat with dough". The earliest known written recipe close to it comes from 13th century Aleppo, and the Ottoman traveller Evliya Çelebi listed it among the notable foods of Damascus. Today it is loved across Turkey, Armenia, Lebanon and Syria, with regional styles: Antep lahmacun uses garlic, Urfa uses onion, and Aleppo style adds pomegranate molasses. In 2025 the European Union registered Gaziantep lahmacun as a protected geographical indication.

Where to eat it in Malta

We only list places where lahmacun is on the menu today. There is no official ranking for this, so the order below is ours: how widely you can find it, then what diners say. Kebab Factory is a featured restaurant on CompareMalta.ai.

4

Kebab Factory

Easiest to findMsida, St Julian's, Paceville, Valletta, Ħamrun, San Ġwann, Mellieħa · Turkish · 7 branches

A Turkish chain founded in Malta in 2019, with lahmacun as a core part of a menu built around döner, kebabs, Turkish pizza and baklava. Diners at the Msida and St Julian's branches single out the lahmacun, and with seven branches it is the simplest way to try it wherever you are on the island.

5

Moo's Kebab

Diners' favouriteGżira (The Strand), Valletta · Turkish · 4.5★ from 571 Tripadvisor reviews

Lahmacun sits in the pide section of the menu next to Turkish-style pizzas. The Gżira branch on The Strand is one of the best reviewed Turkish places on the island, with 4.5 out of 5 from 571 reviews on Tripadvisor, and there is a second branch in Valletta.

Map ↗
Photos: Freddyolsson, CC BY-SA 4.0, Wikimedia Commons
6

Kebab Bistro

Classic recipeSliema (153 Tower Road) · Turkish · Tower Road

A Turkish bistro on Tower Road whose lahmacun follows the classic mix of minced beef, tomato, onion, garlic, parsley and herbs, and is one of the popular items on its delivery menu. Good for a sit-down Turkish meal in Sliema with soups, pides and grills alongside.

Map ↗
Photos: Txllxt TxllxT, CC BY-SA 4.0, Wikimedia Commons

Tip: order it with lemon and fresh herbs and eat it straight away, while the base is still crisp. Menus and opening hours change, so check with the restaurant before you go.

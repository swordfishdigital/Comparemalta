Malta's big five banks on your phone: how their apps really score

We scored the apps of BOV, HSBC, APS, BNF and Lombard on onboarding, everyday use, features and wallets. The best reaches 3.8 out of 5; most sit at 2 or below.

By CompareMalta.ai

Photos: HLundgaard, CC BY-SA 3.0, Wikimedia Commons

Most people in Malta still bank with one of five traditional banks. All five have an app, but having an app and offering a phone-first experience are very different things. Today many people expect to open an account from the sofa, see every card and account on one screen, freeze a card in a tap and pay with their phone.

We scored each bank from 0 to 5 on four things, using only what is public: the App Store listing in Malta (rating, description, recent reviews), Apple's own list of Apple Pay banks in Malta and the banks' own websites, all checked on 22 November 2026. We did not test the apps with live accounts, so read this as a comparison of what each bank offers and how customers rate it.

Bank by bank

APS Bank · myAPS

Best of the five3.8 / 5 · 4.5★ from 132 ratings (App Store Malta)

The most complete of the five. Existing customers can open new accounts in the app, pay by mobile number, change debit card limits and monitor investments, and APS says it was the first local bank to drop online SEPA payment fees. Cards work with Apple Pay and Google Wallet. The gap: a brand new customer still has to become an APS customer and sign up through the website before the app is any use.

Bank of Valletta · BOV Mobile

Strong features, weak reviews3.0 / 5 · 2.6★ from 233 ratings (App Store Malta)

The feature list is solid: Stop A Card for lost cards, payments to friends by mobile number, top-ups and bill payments, and an in-app link to add cards to Apple Pay. But the rating is the lowest of the apps with a score, and recent reviews describe missing accounts and login trouble. New customers can apply online only for a personal savings account, through a separate website, not the app.

HSBC Malta

Best rated, least open2.0 / 5 · 4.7★ from about 12,000 ratings (App Store Malta)

Customers rate it highly and it does the basics reliably: instant SEPA payments to new payees, Global Accounts, approving card payments online and managing open banking consents. But it is for existing online banking customers only, bills and third-party payees mostly have to be set up first, and HSBC Malta is not on Apple's list of Apple Pay banks in Malta.

BNF Bank · BNF Mobile App

Best onboarding2.0 / 5 · No rating shown on the App Store Malta

BNF is the only one of the five where a new customer can open an account 24/7 from a phone or tablet with a Maltese ID or residence card, launched in 2024. After that the app is basic: balances, own transfers, quick payments, bills and secure messages. Too few ratings to show a score, recent reviews report crashes, and BNF is not on Apple's Apple Pay list for Malta.

Lombard Bank

Branch first0.5 / 5 · Mobile app works as a security token

Lombard's internet banking runs from its website, and the bank's own FAQ presents its mobile app as the way to get a software token that generates security codes. To sign up for internet banking you are asked to visit a branch. It is a secure, traditional set-up, but there is little here for someone who wants to bank from a phone.

Map ↗

The pattern is clear: Malta's traditional banks have added apps on top of branch processes rather than building around the phone. Only one advertises opening an account end to end from a phone, only two appear on Apple's Apple Pay list for Malta, and none of the five store listings mention in-app spending insights or virtual cards.

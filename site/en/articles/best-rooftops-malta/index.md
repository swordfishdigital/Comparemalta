# Malta's eight best rooftops, from Valletta to Mellieħa

Harbour sunsets, infinity pools and the island's highest views: eight rooftop bars and terraces worth the lift ride, in order of a trip from Valletta up the coast.

## Our eight

1. **Over Grain** (Valletta classic), Valletta · Rosselli AX Privilege, 4th floor: Above the Michelin-starred Under Grain, this is one of the most elegant drinks in the capital, with views over Valletta's domes and out to sea. Come at sunset and stay as the city lights up.

2. **The Rooftop at The Embassy** (Pool in the capital), Valletta · 8th floor: A rooftop restaurant and lounge with a pool on top of Valletta, city and sea views, and a degustation menu if you want to make an evening of it.

3. **Lounge 360 & TemptAsian** (Best Valletta view), Sliema · AX The Palace, 9th floor: One of the best-known terraces on the island, looking across Sliema's rooftops and the sea. By day there is an infinity pool; at sunset it turns into a lounge with sushi and Asian fusion.

4. **The Skyroom** (Sea views on Tower Road), Sliema · The Preluna, 13th to 14th floor: High above the Tower Road promenade, with wide sea and island views and a grill menu, so it works for dinner as well as drinks.

5. **SKYBEACH** (360° views), St Julian's · InterContinental Malta: On top of the InterContinental, with 360° views over St Julian's and the coast. A pool club by day and a glamorous lounge by night.

6. **Infinity by Hugo's** (Most luxurious), St Julian's · Hugo's Boutique Hotel, 11th floor: A luxury rooftop with a long infinity pool and hot tubs above St Julian's, plus international dishes and champagne. The place for a special occasion.

7. **Taza Sky Bar** (Lowest cocktail prices), Qawra · db San Antonio, 10th floor: Views over St Paul's Bay and the open sea from the tenth floor, with an infinity pool and the lowest cocktail prices of these eight in The Rooftop Guide's listing.

8. **Zendeck & Topdeck** (Best sunset), Mellieħa · Solana Hotel & Spa, 7th floor: The northern finale: views over Għadira Bay, Mellieħa village and the Red Tower, an adults-only infinity pool, and Asian fusion in the evening.

Rooftop pools are often for hotel guests or open only in season, and dress codes and minimum spends vary, so book or call ahead. For dinner ideas below the rooftops, see Restaurants and Blue's picks on CompareMalta.ai.

# Malta's three best supermarket experiences

Late hours and calm aisles, the cheapest big weekly shop, or the lowest prices on own brands: the three supermarkets that get the experience right, and who each one suits.

## Blue's top three

1. **Greens** (Best overall experience), Swieqi, Mrieħel, Qormi, Gozo: All four stores open from 7am to midnight every day, public holidays included, so the shop fits around your life rather than the other way round. The range is the widest of the three, with imported brands and organic lines, and Calm Hours turn off music and screens on selected afternoons. The trade-off is price: guides put it in the higher-end tier.

2. **PAVI / PAMA** (Best big weekly shop), Qormi, Mosta: Two big stores with deli, butcher and bakery counters, and PAVI's fish counter is singled out as one of the best on the island. In an independent 10-item basket comparison from July 2026 it was the cheapest of the four stores checked, at €34.16. A shared loyalty card works in both, and PAMA in Mosta is a full shopping village.

3. **Lidl** (Best for low prices), 10 stores, 9 in Malta and 1 in Gozo: Guides rate Lidl's prices as the best overall, with baskets around 20 to 30% below mid-tier chains, and strong own brands in dairy, bread, frozen food and basics. Since 30 April 2026 the Lidl Plus app also earns points you can swap for free products. Expect a narrower choice of international brands and stock that comes and goes, so check the weekly offers before you go.

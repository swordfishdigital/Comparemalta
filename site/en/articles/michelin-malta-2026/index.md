# Every MICHELIN star and Bib Gourmand in Malta, ranked

Malta's 2026 MICHELIN Guide keeps all seven starred tables and adds a fifth Bib Gourmand. Here are all twelve, from ION Harbour's two stars to the best value plates on the islands.

## Two MICHELIN Stars

1. **ION Harbour by Simon Rogan** (2 Stars), Valletta: Malta's only two-star restaurant, and it has held both stars for three editions in a row. Simon Rogan's team builds the tasting menu around produce from small Maltese growers, served with a view over the Grand Harbour.

## One MICHELIN Star

2. **De Mondion** (1 Star), Mdina: On the roof of the Xara Palace inside the walls of Mdina, with views across the island. Refined classic cooking that leans on Maltese tradition. Its sommelier, Miljan Radonjić, won the 2026 MICHELIN Sommelier Award.

3. **Fernandõ Gastrotheque** (1 Star), Sliema: A small, two-floor dining room on a quiet Sliema side street, starred since 2023. Short, seasonal menus and one of the island's most serious wine lists, with more than 700 labels.

4. **Le GV** (1 Star), Sliema: The rooftop restaurant of the 1926 Le Soleil hotel, and the newest star on the islands: it earned it in 2025 and kept it this year. Book for sunset over the Sliema skyline.

5. **Noni** (1 Star), Valletta: A relaxed tasting menu served under the stone vaults of a converted underground bakery in the capital. One of Malta's first starred restaurants, holding its star since the guide launched in 2020.

6. **Rosamì** (1 Star), St Julian's: Seasonal Mediterranean tasting menus in a historic villa above Balluta Bay. Starred since 2024 and one of the most romantic settings on the list.

7. **Under Grain** (1 Star), Valletta: The fine dining room of the Grain group, below street level in Valletta's old merchants' quarter. Starred since 2020; its sister Grain Street, upstairs, holds a Bib Gourmand.

## Bib Gourmand: great food, fair prices

8. **AYU** (Bib Gourmand), Gżira: Three brothers cook dishes shaped by their travels, opposite Manoel Island on the Gżira promenade. Laid back and easy to love after a seafront walk.

9. **Commando** (Bib Gourmand), Mellieħa: A family restaurant in the village square that has welcomed guests since the 1930s. A young chef-owner keeps the hospitality and gives fresh island produce a light modern touch.

10. **Grain Street** (Bib Gourmand), Valletta: The relaxed street-level bistro of the Grain group on Merchants Street, with an outdoor terrace. Sharing plates from the same kitchen culture as starred Under Grain, at a fraction of the price.

11. **Rubino** (Bib Gourmand), Valletta: A tiny Valletta institution that opened in 1906 as a confectioner's shop, its windows still lined with old jars and bottles. Honest Maltese cooking, and a Bib Gourmand since the very first guide.

12. **Verbena** (Bib Gourmand · New), Mġarr: New to the list in 2026. Honest, generous cooking built on farm-fresh ingredients in the quiet agricultural village of Mġarr, a short drive from the north coast beaches.

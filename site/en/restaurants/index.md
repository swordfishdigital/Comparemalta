## How we rank

First we hide products that do not fit your needs (amount, term, deposit, currency) and never rank different kinds of product together; then the lowest total cost wins. The Blue score (0 to 10) summarises this for the default view and weighs: Rating 80%, reviews 20%. Nobody can pay for a better position.

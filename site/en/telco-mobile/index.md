## How we rank

First we hide products that do not fit your needs (amount, term, deposit, currency) and never rank different kinds of product together; then the lowest total cost wins. The Blue score (0 to 10) summarises this for the default view and weighs: Per month 50%, Data (GB) 35%, EU roaming (GB) 15%. Nobody can pay for a better position.

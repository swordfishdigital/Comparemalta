How CompareMalta.ai ranks products

Every comparison uses public prices from each provider's own website, refreshed by our feeds every day. We turn them into a Blue score from 0 to 10.

  • Step 1, eligibility: products that do not fit your amount, term, deposit or currency are hidden, and different kinds of product (12 and 24 month deposits, fixed and variable mortgages, car and home insurance) are never ranked together.
  • Step 2, cost: among what fits, the lowest total cost for your numbers wins. For card payments (POS) you enter your own monthly sales, average sale and card mix.
  • Step 3, preferences: speed, app and hardware count only after cost.
  • Insurance has no overall score, because cover differs.
  • Each category has its own weights, shown on every comparison page.
  • Missing values count as the category median, so a gap never helps or hurts much.
  • Providers cannot pay to rank higher. Partnerships never change the order.
  • Sample figures are labelled until a live feed for that provider is connected.
